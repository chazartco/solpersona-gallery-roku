# Changelog

## 1.0.3 — first store release

- **Roku Channel Store:** scheduled to publish worldwide on October 19, 2026
  at 10:00 AM Pacific.
- **Source:** tag `v1.0.3` (commit `8daee71`).
- **Signed package SHA-256:**
  `87f3e250591592468097eb669676e33eaed4d45d66a68e87e5bcebe7a43f2f2d`
- **Minimum firmware:** Roku OS 15.1 (RSG 1.3).
- **Static Analysis:** no findings.
- **Soak tests** (Roku Ultra 4800X, OS 16.0.4): 1.0.3 for 2 h, and 1.0.2 for
  6 h 26 min. No crashes, memory steady at about 30 MiB.

What it does: links a Roku to a SolPersona account by QR code or pairing
code, then plays the chosen gallery with SolPersona's framing, transitions,
labels, films, opening hours and takeovers, in step with other screens.
**Try a sample gallery** works without an account. Owners turn off the Roku
screensaver for an always-on wall.
